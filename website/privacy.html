Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2025

Pacelab (“we”, “our”, or “the app”) is a running and training coach app. This policy explains how we collect, use, and disclose your data, including your activity data (workouts, runs, health and fitness data). By using Pacelab, you agree to this policy.

1. Who is responsible for your data?

The data controller for Pacelab is the operator of the Pacelab app and this website. You can contact us using the details at the end of this policy.

2. Activity and fitness data we collect

We collect and process data that relates to your physical activity and use of the app, including:

  • Workout and run data: distance, duration, pace, speed, heart rate, route (GPS), elevation, and similar metrics.
  • Health and fitness data: when you connect Apple Health (HealthKit), we may receive workouts, heart rate, steps, and other data you choose to share.
  • Data from linked services: if you connect Strava or Garmin, we receive activity and profile data those services make available to us under their terms and your permissions.
  • Account and usage data: account information, how you use the app (e.g. screens and features used), and device-related identifiers where necessary for the service.

3. How we use your activity data

We use your activity and related data to:

  • Provide coaching, training plans, and personalized advice.
  • Analyze your runs and workouts (e.g. pace, heart rate, routes) to give you feedback and recommendations.
  • Power in-app features such as maps, charts, and AI-driven insights.
  • Improve the app, fix errors, and ensure security.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We do not use your activity data for advertising or to build advertising profiles. We do not sell your personal or activity data.

4. How we disclose your activity data

We may disclose your data (including activity data) only as follows:

  • Service providers: we use providers that process data on our behalf (e.g. hosting, databases, AI for coaching). They are bound by contracts that limit use to providing our service and protecting your data.
  • Linked services: if you connect Strava, Garmin, or similar services, data may be sent to and from those services according to their privacy policies and your choices.
  • Legal: we may disclose data when required by law or to protect our or others’ rights and safety.

We do not share or sell your activity data to data brokers or for third-party marketing.

5. Storage, security, and retention

Your data is stored on secure servers and we use industry-standard measures to protect it. We keep your data only as long as needed to provide the service, comply with law, or enforce our terms. You can request deletion of your account and associated data (see “Your rights” below).

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access and receive a copy of your data.
  • Correct inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your data and account.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Data portability (e.g. receive your data in a machine-readable format).

To exercise these rights or ask questions about your activity data, contact us (see below). You may also have the right to complain to a data protection authority.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the current version on this page and, where appropriate, notify you in the app or by email. The “Last updated” date at the top indicates the latest version.

8. Contact

For privacy-related requests, questions about how we use or disclose your activity data, or to exercise your rights, contact us at the support or contact option provided in the Pacelab app or on this website.
